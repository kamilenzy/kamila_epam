Methodology and limitations

The report is split by numbered use-case headings (sections 2–7). For each term, case-insensitive whole-word or phrase patterns are counted within each section. Explicit patterns match canonical terms and direct abbreviations; implicit patterns match curated descriptions that express a related concept without naming it. A phrase match is counted once at its starting position. Implicit patterns are not allowed to duplicate an explicit match of the same term. Because this is rule-based, some paraphrases may be missed, and ambiguous phrases may be counted incorrectly. Counts can be audited in the JSON export. The analysis excludes the introduction, cross-cutting considerations, conclusion, and references to focus on application-specific relationships.
